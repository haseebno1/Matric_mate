# 2-3 Minute Demo Video Script: Matric AI Study Planner

**App Name:** Matric AI - Adaptive Study Assistant  
**Target Duration:** 2 minutes 30 seconds (150 seconds)  
**Format:** Screen Recording with Voiceover (Mobile View or Mobile Emulator Frame)

---

## Video Timeline & Script Breakdown

### ⏱️ 0:00 – 0:25 | Scene 1: Introduction & The Problem
* **Visual on Screen:** Show the mobile phone view loading the app with the **Onboarding Setup Modal**.
* **Voiceover:**  
  > "Hi everyone! Preparing for Matric final exams is one of the most stressful experiences for high school students. Managing 6 or 7 subjects like Mathematics, Physical Sciences, and Accounting with limited time often leads to burnout and poor exam results. Meet **Matric AI** — an intelligent, mobile-first study planner designed to eliminate study stress using artificial intelligence."

---

### ⏱️ 0:25 – 0:55 | Scene 2: Personalised Onboarding & AI Adaptive Timetable
* **Visual on Screen:**  
  1. Complete the onboarding modal: Select Grade 12, choose subjects (Maths, Physics, Accounting), adjust confidence sliders (e.g. Maths = 2/5, Physics = 4/5).
  2. Click **"Save Profile & Generate Schedule"**.
  3. Show the generated schedule view automatically populated with prioritized daily study slots.
* **Voiceover:**  
  > "When a student opens the app, they set up their subject profile, target percentage, and rate their confidence in each topic. In just one click, our AI engine synthesizes a personalized daily study timetable. Notice how subjects with lower confidence ratings and closer exam dates are automatically prioritized with dedicated revision slots!"

---

### ⏱️ 0:55 – 1:35 | Scene 3: AI Study Buddy (Instant Doubt Resolution)
* **Visual on Screen:**  
  1. Tap on the **AI Buddy** tab in the mobile navigation.
  2. Select subject "Mathematics" and topic "Quadratic Equations".
  3. Type question: *"How do I solve by completing the square?"*
  4. Hit send and show the AI tutor generating a step-by-step breakdown with exam mark hints and key takeaways.
* **Voiceover:**  
  > "When students get stuck late at night, they don't have to wait for school the next morning. In the AI Study Buddy tab, students can select any subject and ask questions. Powered by Gemini 3.6 Flash, the tutor gives clear, step-by-step breakdowns, highlights where exam marks are awarded, and summarizes key formulas to remember."

---

### ⏱️ 1:35 – 2:05 | Scene 4: AI Diagnostic Quizzes & Auto-Rescheduling
* **Visual on Screen:**  
  1. Tap on **Practice Quiz**.
  2. Start a 5-question quiz for Physical Sciences - Newton's Laws.
  3. Answer questions, submit quiz, and show score screen (e.g. 60%).
  4. Tap **"Auto-Schedule Weak Topics"** button.
  5. Show pop-up toast: *"Priority revision slot added for tomorrow!"*
* **Voiceover:**  
  > "Active recall is proven to be the best way to study. Our AI Quiz engine generates 5 diagnostic questions for any topic. When a student completes a quiz, the AI identifies their weak areas and automatically injects remedial revision slots into their calendar so no concept is left behind!"

---

### ⏱️ 2:05 – 2:30 | Scene 5: Analytics, Notes & Conclusion
* **Visual on Screen:**  
  1. Tap on **Analytics** view to show exam countdown, study hours progress, and confidence heatmap.
  2. Show **Smart Notes** tab with AI takeaway extraction.
  3. End on the clean home dashboard.
* **Voiceover:**  
  > "Students can track their overall progress, streak days, and countdowns to final exams on the visual Analytics dashboard, while managing summarized notes. Matric AI turns study anxiety into structured confidence. Thank you!"

---

## 💡 Recording Tips for Success:
1. **Screen Resolution:** Record in 1080p vertical or standard 16:9 widescreen using OBS, Loom, or mobile screen recorder.
2. **Audio:** Use a clear microphone with zero background noise.
3. **Pacing:** Speak confidently and clearly without rushing.
